Lisa Forsberg's personal academic website.

Lisa Forsberg is Senior Research Fellow at the Uehiro Oxford Institute, University of Oxford and Research Fellow in Philosophy at Somerville College, University of Oxford.

Lisa Forsberg's main research interests and expertise lie in normative and practical ethics, and in the philosophy of medical and criminal law.
